 NCC Portharcourt Raids Piracy Spots, Impounds Contrivances 

 By copyrightgov 

 June 16, 2025 

 0 Comments 

 THE Nigerian Copyright Commission, NCC, Port-Harcourt Office on the 7th of March, 2025 carried out a Broadcast Piracy Raid at two locations in Port-Harcourt Metropolis, Agip Village and Mile 2, Diobu. 
Director, Port-Harcourt Office, Mr. Abdul Salami Babatunde, informed that the raids were carried out following reports of widespread illegal rebroadcasting of signals, belonging to MultiChoice Nigeria. 
Mr. Babatunde added that the Commission’s Operatives seized contrivances used for illegal transmission of signals such as cables, TV sets, decoders, splitters, remote control and smart cards, during the raid . 
The Director noted that no arrests were made but that investigation was ongoing to identify and prosecute perpetrators of the crime. 
Assistant Director, NCC, Port-Harcourt Office, Mr. Joseph Ojika led some Copyright Officers on the raid with armed security backup from the Nigerian Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC).
