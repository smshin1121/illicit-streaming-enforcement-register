# Acquisition log — publisher-index walk for the paper-2 census

Started 2026-08-17. This file is the record a reviewer will ask for when they ask
"how did you search". §3.1 of the manuscript will cite it.

## Why this exists

Until 2026-08-17 the corpus's completeness rested on a June 2026 worldwide sweep
(12 regions, 22 agents, WebSearch-driven) that added two operations and was
recorded as "saturated". Between 2026-06-05 and 2026-08-16 the census then gained
**eight** rows, four of them announced in **2017 and 2018** — found not by fresh
news but by walking one national prosecutor's press index (justiz.bayern.de)
during the domestic-repository work. Walking one Greek police index produced 28
domestic actions. Coverage was therefore a function of *which publisher indices
had been walked*, not of the world, and the June verdict was scoped to a stricter
population (≥2 sovereign LE) than the paper's. A WebSearch sweep discovers what a
search engine ranks; an index walk discovers what a publisher published.

## Protocol (fixed before the walk)

- **Unit of search**: a tier-1 publisher's own press/news index — agency,
  prosecutor, court, IGO, or rights-holder coalition. Not search engines.
- **Window**: announced 2014-01-01 .. 2026-08-17. This is also the register's
  census window (`REGISTER.md` predicate 6). ⚠ Until 2026-08-17 this line said
  "the paper reports 2017–2026" — that was the measured span of the 67-action
  wiki census, and the register holds 18 census rows dated 2014–2016; the
  manuscript reframe states the window it uses (codex IC reg1).
- **Object**: an enforcement action against illicit IPTV / streaming / pay-TV /
  cardsharing infrastructure or its sellers — arrests, searches, seizures,
  takedowns, blocking orders, indictments, convictions. Both cooperative (the
  IC repository) and domestic (the DE repository) actions are collected; the
  stratum is assigned afterwards by one rule applied to the source. Excluded
  by the paper's medium layer: manga/webtoon/text-image piracy, P2P release
  groups, pre-release intrusion rings, and device offences with no unauthorised
  content-access element stated. Sellers of set-top boxes marketed for access
  to unauthorised streams ARE in scope (`medium = isd-retail`; the Hong Kong
  Customs box-shop raids are census rows) — until 2026-08-17 this line said
  "pure ISD retail without a service" while `REGISTER.md` included box
  sellers; the two files now say the same thing (codex IC reg1).
- **Per publisher, log**: index URL · what range was walked (pages / years /
  filters) · fetch rung that worked · hits (candidate URLs) · blocked or not.
  **Zero hits is a result and is logged** — a walked-and-empty publisher is
  the coverage claim.
- **Per candidate, log**: publisher · publish date (from the release, not a
  snippet — L47) · title in original language · URL · medium · countries
  named in the release · organisations credited by name (not "a copyright
  owner") · dedup verdict against both repositories with the grep patterns
  used · notes.
- **Dedup**: `grep -ril <service name | distinctive figure | date>` over
  `wiki/operations`, `wiki/sources`, `wiki/cases` here and `wiki/actions` in
  `../Cyber-crime-DE`. Verdicts: `new` / `possible-dup:<slug>` / `dup:<slug>`.
- **Stopping rule**: the walk ends when every publisher on the list below has
  a log line. It does not end when candidates stop appearing.
- **What this pass does NOT do**: ingest. Candidates are adjudicated
  afterwards against the boundary rules and ingested one at a time with their
  own capture metadata.
